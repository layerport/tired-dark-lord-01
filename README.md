A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.
The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections —
never in a plain statement of weariness. The speaker is a specific villain:
a usurper who signed three kings' deaths with a quill stolen from his own
brother, who still writes him every week, and who is waiting out tonight's
eclipse with fed ravens and a shut gate.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the
monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
